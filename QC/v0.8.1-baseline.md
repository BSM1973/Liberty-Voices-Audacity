## Peak and loudness
Both CI jobs measured -1.5 dB interpolated on the limited synthetic sinusoid at 44.1/48/96 kHz, ceiling -1 dB; same interpolator for detector and test. Not independent TP certification.
Additional Linux C++ harness copied the WeightFilter, LoudnessMeter and Interpolation classes from baseline; recreated EBU Tech 3341 Table 1 cases, not official WAVs:
I cases 1-5 = -22.9933, -32.9933, -23.0139, -23.0139, -22.9787 LUFS.
Peak cases 15-19 = -6.0206, -6.0184, -6.0217, -6.0189, +2.9866 dB estimated.
All ten within case tolerances. New CTest target uses actual project headers; platform results pending.
References: https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf and https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/ebu_loudness_test_set

- Long repeated full-chain benchmarks at 32/64/128/256, including mastering and four harmony formants; investigate 32-sample Studio deadline miss.
- No commercial release or merge until reviewed.
